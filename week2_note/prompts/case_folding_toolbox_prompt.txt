Create a complex articulated folding toolbox for testing MP4 motion export.

The toolbox should have:
- a rectangular dark blue plastic base,
- a large top lid,
- two side trays,
- two front drawers,
- gray metal hinges,
- visible drawer rails,
- and small handles so the motion axes are easy to understand.

Coordinate convention:
- X is left-right width.
- Y is front-back depth.
- Z is vertical height.
- Front is toward negative Y.
- Back is toward positive Y.
- Left is negative X.
- Right is positive X.

Use simple box geometry only, but make the object visually clear and detailed enough for MP4 motion testing.

Parts:
1. toolbox_base: fixed root part.
2. top_lid: large hinged lid.
3. left_tray: hinged side tray opening to the left.
4. right_tray: hinged side tray opening to the right.
5. upper_front_drawer: sliding upper front drawer.
6. lower_front_drawer: sliding lower front drawer.

Toolbox base:
- Make a dark blue rectangular toolbox base.
- It should look like a plastic storage toolbox.
- Add front drawer openings.
- Add visible gray drawer rails on both sides of each drawer.
- Add small gray hinge plates on the rear side for the top lid.
- Add small gray side hinge plates for the left and right trays.
- Keep the base as the fixed root part.

Top lid:
- Large dark blue lid on top of the toolbox.
- Use a REVOLUTE joint.
- Parent: toolbox_base.
- Child: top_lid.
- The lid should rotate upward and backward around a rear hinge.
- Axis should be along the X axis.
- Hinge origin should be at the rear top edge of the toolbox, not at the lid center.
- Motion limits: lower=0, upper about 1.35 radians.
- Add a small front handle on the lid.
- Add gray hinge details along the rear edge.

Left tray:
- A side tray on the left side of the toolbox.
- Use a REVOLUTE joint.
- Parent: toolbox_base.
- Child: left_tray.
- It should rotate outward to the left around a side hinge.
- Axis should be vertical, along the Z axis.
- Hinge origin should be on the left side edge of the toolbox.
- Motion limits: lower=0, upper about 1.35 radians.
- Add a small handle or lip.
- Add gray hinge plates so the rotation axis is visible.

Right tray:
- A side tray on the right side of the toolbox.
- Use a REVOLUTE joint.
- Parent: toolbox_base.
- Child: right_tray.
- It should rotate outward to the right around a side hinge.
- Axis should be vertical, along the Z axis.
- Hinge origin should be on the right side edge of the toolbox.
- Motion limits: lower=-1.35, upper=0 if needed, or use a positive direction that clearly opens outward to the right.
- Add a small handle or lip.
- Add gray hinge plates so the rotation axis is visible.

Upper front drawer:
- A drawer in the upper front part of the toolbox.
- Use a PRISMATIC joint.
- Parent: toolbox_base.
- Child: upper_front_drawer.
- It should slide forward toward negative Y.
- Axis: (0, -1, 0).
- Motion limits: lower=0, upper about 0.22.
- Add a small front handle.
- Add visible side rails.
- Keep enough spacing so it does not visually merge with the lower drawer.

Lower front drawer:
- A drawer in the lower front part of the toolbox.
- Use a PRISMATIC joint.
- Parent: toolbox_base.
- Child: lower_front_drawer.
- It should slide forward toward negative Y.
- Axis: (0, -1, 0).
- Motion limits: lower=0, upper about 0.25.
- Add a small front handle.
- Add visible side rails.
- Keep enough spacing so it is clearly separate from the upper drawer.

Motion sequence requirement for MP4 export:
- The motion should happen one part at a time.
- First, the top lid rotates upward and backward around the rear hinge.
- Keep the lid open.
- Then the left tray rotates outward to the left around its side hinge.
- Keep it open.
- Then the right tray rotates outward to the right around its side hinge.
- Keep it open.
- Then the upper front drawer slides forward on a prismatic joint.
- Keep it extended.
- Finally, the lower front drawer slides forward on a prismatic joint.

Important visual requirements:
- Make each moving part clearly visible and separated from the others.
- Use realistic proportions.
- Use clear hinge axes.
- Add enough spacing so every motion is visible in the exported MP4.
- Avoid parts intersecting during motion.
- Avoid placing hinge origins at the center of panels.
- The object should be suitable for testing sequential MP4 export.
