Create a simple openable drawer as an articulated object.

The object should be a small cabinet with one pull-out drawer.

Coordinate convention:
- X is left-right width.
- Y is front-back depth.
- Z is vertical height.
- The drawer should open by sliding forward along the negative Y direction.

Parts:
1. A fixed cabinet frame as the root part.
2. One movable drawer tray as the child part.

Cabinet frame requirements:
- Use simple box geometry.
- The frame should include left wall, right wall, top panel, bottom panel, and back panel.
- Leave the front open so the drawer can slide out.
- Add two side rails or guide rails inside the cabinet to visually support the drawer.

Drawer requirements:
- The drawer should not be a solid block.
- It should look like an open tray with:
  - bottom panel,
  - left side wall,
  - right side wall,
  - back wall,
  - front panel,
  - handle on the front panel.
- The drawer should fit inside the cabinet in the closed position.
- The drawer front panel should sit just in front of the cabinet opening when closed.
- Avoid interpenetration between the drawer front and the cabinet frame.

Joint requirements:
- Connect the drawer tray to the cabinet frame using one PRISMATIC joint.
- The parent should be the cabinet frame.
- The child should be the drawer tray.
- The joint axis should be (0, -1, 0), so the drawer slides forward.
- The closed position should be 0.
- The open position should be around 0.28 meters.
- Use motion limits lower=0 and upper=0.28.

Validation tests:
- Check that the cabinet frame is the root part.
- Check that there is exactly one prismatic joint.
- Check that the joint parent is the cabinet frame and the child is the drawer tray.
- Check that the joint axis points along negative Y.
- Check that the joint limits allow the drawer to move from 0 to about 0.28.
- Check that the drawer is inside the cabinet when closed.
- Check that the drawer moves forward when opened.
- Check that the drawer remains supported by the side rails in the open position.
- Check that the drawer front does not collide with the cabinet frame in the closed position.

Use clear part names and visual element names.
