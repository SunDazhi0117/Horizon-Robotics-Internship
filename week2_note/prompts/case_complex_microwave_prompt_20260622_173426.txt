Create a complex articulated microwave oven as an articulated object.

This object should be moderately complex and useful for debugging articulated object generation.

Coordinate convention:
- X is left-right width.
- Y is front-back depth.
- Z is vertical height.
- The front of the microwave is toward negative Y.
- The back of the microwave is toward positive Y.

Overall object:
Create a countertop microwave oven with:
1. A fixed microwave body as the root part.
2. A hinged front door that opens outward.
3. A sliding inner tray that can be pulled forward.
4. A continuously rotating turntable on the tray.
5. Two rotary control knobs on the right control panel.

Use simple box geometry only. If a round object is needed, approximate it with multiple thin boxes.

Parts:
- microwave_body: fixed root part.
- front_door: movable hinged door.
- sliding_tray: movable tray inside the microwave.
- turntable: continuously rotating plate on the tray.
- upper_knob: continuously rotating upper control knob.
- lower_knob: continuously rotating lower control knob.

Microwave body geometry:
- The body should look like a hollow rectangular microwave frame.
- Include left wall, right wall, top panel, bottom panel, back panel, and internal floor.
- The front should be open.
- Add a right-side control panel area next to the door.
- Add two side rails inside the cavity to support the sliding tray.
- Do not make the microwave body one solid block.

Front door geometry:
- The door should be a thin rectangular door frame with a window area.
- Add a vertical handle on the left side of the door front.
- The door should cover the main oven opening when closed.
- The door should not cover the right control panel.
- The door should be hinged on its right edge next to the control panel.
- The door should open outward toward the viewer.

Sliding tray geometry:
- The tray should sit inside the microwave cavity.
- It should be a thin rectangular plate supported by the side rails.
- It should slide forward along negative Y.
- It should remain partially supported by the rails when opened.
- It should not pass through the front door in the closed position.

Turntable geometry:
- The turntable should sit on top of the sliding tray.
- Approximate a circular plate using simple box geometry.
- Add a small visual marker on top so rotation is visible.
- The turntable should rotate in place around the vertical Z axis.
- It should not orbit around the tray.

Knob geometry:
- Place two control knobs on the right control panel.
- upper_knob should be above lower_knob.
- Each knob should rotate around its own front-back Y axis.
- Add a small indicator mark on each knob.
- The knobs should be attached to the control panel and should not float.

Joints:
1. front_door joint:
   - Type: REVOLUTE.
   - Parent: microwave_body.
   - Child: front_door.
   - Axis: (0, 0, 1).
   - Hinge origin should be along the right vertical edge of the door.
   - Closed position: 0.
   - Open position: about 100 degrees.
   - Motion limits: lower=0, upper about 1.75 radians.
   - The door must rotate around its right edge, not around its center.

2. sliding_tray joint:
   - Type: PRISMATIC.
   - Parent: microwave_body.
   - Child: sliding_tray.
   - Axis: (0, -1, 0).
   - Lower limit: 0.
   - Upper limit: about 0.22 meters.
   - The tray should slide forward out of the microwave cavity.

3. turntable joint:
   - Type: CONTINUOUS.
   - Parent: sliding_tray.
   - Child: turntable.
   - Axis: (0, 0, 1).
   - Joint origin should be at the center of the turntable.
   - No finite lower or upper angular limits.
   - The turntable should rotate in place relative to the tray.

4. upper_knob joint:
   - Type: CONTINUOUS.
   - Parent: microwave_body.
   - Child: upper_knob.
   - Axis should be along the front-back Y axis.
   - Joint origin should be at the center of the upper knob.
   - The knob should rotate in place on the control panel.

5. lower_knob joint:
   - Type: CONTINUOUS.
   - Parent: microwave_body.
   - Child: lower_knob.
   - Axis should be along the front-back Y axis.
   - Joint origin should be at the center of the lower knob.
   - The knob should rotate in place on the control panel.

Validation tests:
- Check that microwave_body is the root part.
- Check that there are exactly five joints.
- Check that front_door uses a REVOLUTE joint.
- Check that sliding_tray uses a PRISMATIC joint.
- Check that turntable uses a CONTINUOUS joint.
- Check that upper_knob and lower_knob use CONTINUOUS joints.
- Check that front_door parent is microwave_body and child is front_door.
- Check that sliding_tray parent is microwave_body and child is sliding_tray.
- Check that turntable parent is sliding_tray and child is turntable.
- Check that the door hinge origin is near the right vertical edge of the door.
- Check that the door opens outward and does not rotate around its center.
- Check that the tray slides forward along negative Y.
- Check that the tray remains supported by the rails when opened.
- Check that the turntable rotates around its own center.
- Check that both knobs rotate around their own centers.
- Check that the knobs remain attached to the control panel.
- Check that the closed door does not interpenetrate the microwave body.
- Check that the tray does not interpenetrate the back wall or front door in the closed position.
- Check that the moving parts are visually separated and not merged into one solid block.

Important:
- Use clear part names.
- Use clear visual element names.
- Prefer readable and debuggable code.
- Add comments explaining key dimensions, joint origins, and axes.
