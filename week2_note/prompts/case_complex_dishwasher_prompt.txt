Create a complex articulated dishwasher as an articulated object.

Coordinate convention:
- X is left-right width.
- Y is front-back depth.
- Z is vertical height.
- Front is toward negative Y.
- Back is toward positive Y.

Use simple box geometry only.

Parts:
1. dishwasher_body: fixed root part.
2. front_door: movable hinged door.
3. upper_rack: movable sliding upper rack.
4. lower_rack: movable sliding lower rack.
5. spray_arm: continuously rotating spray arm.
6. detergent_drawer: small sliding detergent drawer mounted on the inner side of the door.

Dishwasher body:
- Hollow rectangular dishwasher cabinet.
- Include left wall, right wall, top panel, bottom panel, back panel, and inner floor.
- The front should be open.
- Add rail supports for upper_rack and lower_rack.
- Do not make it one solid block.

Front door:
- Thin rectangular panel covering the front opening when closed.
- Hinge along the bottom front edge.
- Use a REVOLUTE joint.
- Parent: dishwasher_body.
- Child: front_door.
- Axis: along X axis.
- Closed position: 0.
- Open position: about 100 degrees.
- Motion limits lower=0, upper about 1.75 radians.
- Door should rotate around bottom edge, not center.
- Add an outer handle near the top front of the door.

Upper rack:
- Basket-like rack, not a solid block.
- Include thin base rails, left/right side rails, front/back rails.
- Use a PRISMATIC joint.
- Parent: dishwasher_body.
- Child: upper_rack.
- Axis: (0, -1, 0).
- Motion limits lower=0, upper about 0.25.
- Rack should remain supported when opened.

Lower rack:
- Similar basket-like rack but lower and slightly larger.
- Use a PRISMATIC joint.
- Parent: dishwasher_body.
- Child: lower_rack.
- Axis: (0, -1, 0).
- Motion limits lower=0, upper about 0.28.
- Rack should remain supported when opened.

Spray arm:
- Near the bottom inside the dishwasher.
- Approximate with two long thin rectangular arms crossing at the center.
- Use a CONTINUOUS joint.
- Parent: dishwasher_body.
- Child: spray_arm.
- Axis: (0, 0, 1).
- Rotate in place around its own center.

Detergent drawer:
- Small rectangular sliding compartment mounted on the inner face of the front door.
- Use a PRISMATIC joint.
- Parent: front_door.
- Child: detergent_drawer.
- Axis along local outward direction of the door.
- Motion limits lower=0, upper about 0.04.
- It should move with the door because its parent is front_door.

Validation tests:
- Check dishwasher_body is root.
- Check there are exactly five joints.
- Check front_door is REVOLUTE.
- Check upper_rack and lower_rack are PRISMATIC.
- Check spray_arm is CONTINUOUS.
- Check detergent_drawer is PRISMATIC.
- Check detergent_drawer parent is front_door.
- Check racks slide along negative Y.
- Check racks remain supported by rails when opened.
- Check door rotates around bottom edge, not center.
- Check spray_arm rotates around its own center.
- Check detergent drawer remains attached to the door.
- Check moving parts are separate and visually clear.

Important:
- Use clear part names.
- Use clear visual element names.
- Add comments explaining dimensions, joint origins, axes, and likely bug-prone areas.
- Prefer readable and debuggable code.
