Create a simple openable drawer as an articulated object.

Coordinate convention:
- X is left-right width.
- Y is front-back depth.
- Z is vertical height.
- The drawer should open by sliding forward along the negative Y direction.

Object structure:
- One fixed cabinet frame as the root part.
- One movable drawer tray as the child part.

Cabinet frame:
- Use simple box geometry.
- Include left wall, right wall, top panel, bottom panel, and back panel.
- The front should be open.
- Add two side guide rails inside the cabinet.

Drawer:
- The drawer should be an open tray, not a solid block.
- Include bottom panel, left side wall, right side wall, back wall, front panel, and handle.
- The drawer should fit inside the cabinet when closed.
- The drawer front panel should sit just outside the cabinet opening when closed.
- Avoid interpenetration between the drawer front and the cabinet frame.

Joint:
- Use exactly one PRISMATIC joint.
- Parent: cabinet frame.
- Child: drawer tray.
- Axis: (0, -1, 0).
- Lower limit: 0.
- Upper limit: 0.28.

Validation tests:
- Check that the cabinet frame is the root part.
- Check that there is exactly one prismatic joint.
- Check that the joint parent is the cabinet frame and child is the drawer tray.
- Check that the joint axis points along negative Y.
- Check that the drawer moves forward when opened.
- Check that the drawer remains supported by the rails.
- Check that the drawer front does not collide with the cabinet frame when closed.
