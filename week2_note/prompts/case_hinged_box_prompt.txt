Create a simple box with an openable hinged lid as an articulated object.

Coordinate convention:
- X is left-right width.
- Y is front-back depth.
- Z is vertical height.
- The lid should open upward by rotating around the back edge of the box.

Object structure:
- One fixed box body as the root part.
- One movable lid as the child part.

Box body:
- Use simple box geometry.
- The box should look like an open container.
- Include bottom panel, left wall, right wall, front wall, and back wall.
- Leave the top open.

Lid:
- Use a thin rectangular box as the lid panel.
- Add a small handle on the front side of the lid.
- The lid should cover the top opening in the closed position.
- The lid should be hinged at the back edge of the box.
- Avoid interpenetration with the box walls.

Joint:
- Use exactly one REVOLUTE joint.
- Parent: box body.
- Child: lid.
- The hinge origin should be located along the back upper edge of the box.
- The hinge axis should be along the X axis.
- Closed position: 0.
- Open position: about 100 degrees.
- Use motion limits lower=0 and upper about 1.75 radians.

Validation tests:
- Check that the box body is the root part.
- Check that there is exactly one revolute joint.
- Check that the joint parent is the box body and child is the lid.
- Check that the hinge origin is near the back upper edge.
- Check that the lid rotates around the back edge, not around its center.
- Check that the lid covers the box in the closed pose.
- Check that the lid opens upward in the open pose.
